## Keep it honest

- This recording is edited for time. Processing waits may be accelerated
  without extra labels, as the presenter requested. Do not imply real-time
  latency or hide errors.
- No fictional-data disclaimer, speed indicator, result heading, or footer.
  Use neutral roles and exclude real account details.
- Record identifiers are blurred in the Chat excerpts. Real account-owner
  fields are outside the captured regions. Business facts and responses
  are not rewritten.
- The recorded Chat follow-up returned one task, not all four. Do not
  describe that answer as a complete task inventory.
- Cowork initially misstated a secondary task's assignee. The recorded
  verification response corrects it; the edit keeps that correction visible.
  Cowork supplied record names and IDs rather than direct hyperlinks.
- Business Applications in Work IQ and the Cowork plugin retain preview
  wording. Availability does not imply every tenant has access.
- Policy approval is a prerequisite, not an action performed by these notes.
  Request access is not approval. A listing is not a working connection.
- The app evidence and glossary were prepared earlier. No off/on toggles,
  regeneration, or reseeding for filming.
- Validate Chat and Cowork independently. Old Builder/Studio footage and
  the developer-led audio rehearsal are not substitute evidence.
- "Nothing is saved or sent" requires actual read-only behavior and baseline
  verification. If anything changes unexpectedly, stop and reconcile it.
- A proposal is not a saved task or approval. Skill execution, schedules,
  writes, and external sharing require their own evidence and authorization.
- Re-time narration and subtitles to the new cut. Do not speed up the voice
  or alter product responses to fit a proposed window.
