## Steps

### 1. Put your business app to work (0:00-0:30)

- **Tag:** Business context
- **Surface:** Microsoft 365, Power Platform, and Power Apps
- **Value:** Use an existing model-driven app as business context in Copilot, without building a custom agent.
- **0:01 Reuse your investment:** start with the app your team already maintains
- **0:04 Make the app available:** enable Copilot in the app designer
- **0:12 Connect business context:** enable Work IQ for the environment and business data access in Copilot
- **0:26 Work where you ask:** use Copilot instead of opening the app for every review
- **Result 0:27:** [teal] Business records become usable Copilot context

### 2. Understand what the status really means (0:30-1:00)

- **Tag:** Source-backed Chat
- **Surface:** Launch Control Demo and Copilot Chat
- **Value:** Connect a saved business status with the conversations and documents that explain it.
- **0:31 Start from the facts:** ground the discussion in the business record
- **0:37 Less context switching:** connect the record with the team's conversations
- **0:54 Easier to trust:** follow the evidence behind the answer
- **Result 0:57:** [teal] Less searching, one source-backed review

### 3. Write back to your business app (1:00-1:30)

- **Tag:** From insight to action
- **Surface:** Cowork, Business apps (preview)
- **Value:** Delegate analysis and a reviewed update to Cowork, with the next step recorded and tracked in Dataverse.
- **1:01 Enable delegation:** connect Cowork through the Business apps plugin
- **1:11 Move beyond read-only:** let Cowork analyze the issue and propose the update
- **1:15 Stay in control:** review the exact change before it is saved
- **1:24 Close the loop:** verify the next step recorded in Dataverse
- **Result 1:26:** [teal] A next step saved back to the business record

### 4. Bring the next review to you (1:30-2:00)

- **Tag:** Follow-up in Outlook
- **Surface:** Cowork Automations and Outlook
- **Value:** Automate future reviews with connected work and business context, then receive updates without leaving your workflow.
- **1:31 Automate going forward:** schedule the next review in Cowork
- **1:40 Context stays connected:** bring business records and workplace context into the review
- **1:50 Stay in your workflow:** let Cowork deliver the update to your email
- **Result 1:56:** [teal] The review comes to where you already work

## Close

1. Bring your business records together with Microsoft 365 context in Copilot.
2. Drive reviewed updates back into Dataverse and receive scheduled briefings.
3. Worldwide preview rollout in Microsoft Copilot and Cowork.

## Production notes

- Final edition is exactly 2:10: four 30-second steps and a 10-second close.
- The builder's one-second opening card is removed before narration. The
  audience edition starts immediately with step 1, without opening cards.
- Recorded navigation and response reviews are sped up with presenter
  approval. Footage is edited and accelerated, not a real-time performance
  demonstration. No additional speed labels are used, consistent with the
  existing label policy. Original takes and long editions remain available.
- Chat reviews the successful saved launch-level answer, not a newly
  generated answer. The answer and received email are unchanged.
- The scheduled run required attended read approvals. No new schedule or
  email was created for this edit.
  Scheduling is not presented as verified unattended recurring execution.
  Connected context is not a guarantee of zero platform indexing latency
  or unrestricted access.
- Section 3 is a new live pickup. Cowork stages and saves only the launch
  risk summary, then reads it back. The app independently shows the saved
  follow-up. This does not complete the blocked task or request evidence.
  Incidental account and owner names are blurred. The single changed field
  is restored to its captured baseline after recording.
- Section 3 now opens with previously recorded Cowork plugin configuration.
  The Business apps plugin is already enabled and connected in this footage;
  it does not show a new installation or connection. The later write-back
  pickup is a separate task. No live settings are changed for this edit.
- The shorter edition shows highlights, not a readable transcript of every
  answer. Narration and tracker carry the central point while detailed
  records and source lists pass at accelerated speed.
- Launch Control is the example of a reusable business-process pattern,
  not a prerequisite. The demonstrated source is a Dataverse model-driven
  app, not a claim that every business app is automatically supported.
- The opening is reordered: populated app, app-designer Copilot setting,
  environment Work IQ setting, tenant Copilot business-data setting, then
  Copilot Chat. Enabled settings are inspected, not changed for this edit.
  This is not a claim the native app will never be needed again.
- The outro uses a live documentation-page scroll to the Work IQ data
  diagram, replacing the received-email hold only after the demo ends.
  A slow editorial zoom moves toward the diagram after the scroll.
- Availability wording describes a worldwide preview rollout, not confirmed
  completion in every tenant. Microsoft's announcement says rollout continues
  through October; current Learn pages still identify the feature as preview.
  Access remains subject to supported apps, licensing, permissions, and setup.
