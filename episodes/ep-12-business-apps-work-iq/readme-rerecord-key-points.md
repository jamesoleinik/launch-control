## Production notes

- Window-only footage, not screenshot holds. The title cards and closing
  hold are deliberate panel-edition bookends.
- Portal loads, repeated idle reading, and Cowork's internal read approvals
  and lookup steps are edited for time. No performance or latency claim is
  made. The Chat pickup reviews the successful rehearsal's saved prompt,
  full answer, and Sources with real scrolling. Generation is not shown.
  No answer is rewritten or combined with a different run's generation.
- The scheduled run requires attended Business apps read approvals. This
  recording does not demonstrate unattended execution.
- Prepared fictional records and communications existed before capture.
- Chat compares launch-level status and risk with communications, not task
  retrieval. Its handoff email is a draft; the later readiness briefing was
  received from the earlier scheduled demonstration.
- Source-link authentication failure after the loaded Sources scene is
  excluded, not represented as successful record navigation.
- Privacy masks cover incidental account labels and unrelated references.
  They do not replace or rewrite business evidence.
