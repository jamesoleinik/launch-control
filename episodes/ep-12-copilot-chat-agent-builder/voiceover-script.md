## Production boundaries

Keep both prompt-to-answer sequences at original speed. A navigation cut can
remove setup gaps, not response latency. Do not attribute improvements to the
glossary alone, claim business-skill execution, or generalize one successful
answer into a reliability guarantee.

Instructions are not security controls, and selecting knowledge does not grant
Dataverse permissions. This read-only demo adds no writes, approvals, messages,
or schedules. No replacement video, final cue timing, or presenter audio is
verified until capture and media review are complete.
