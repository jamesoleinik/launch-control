## Script

### 1. Put your business app to work (0:00-0:30)

- **0:00** We're finally back to Launch Control. The Business Applications in Work IQ preview brings our existing app into Copilot. I can build on what my team uses.
- **0:10** I enable Copilot in the app designer and Work IQ for the environment. The app's forms and relationships give business data meaning. That understanding now extends into Copilot.
- **0:20** Then I enable business data access in Copilot. I can ask about my business and act on its records without switching apps. My existing permissions still govern that access.

### 2. Understand what the status really means (0:30-1:00)

- **0:30** My launch record says we're at risk. But that status alone doesn't explain what my team knows or where I should focus. For that I need the context.
- **0:40** Copilot brings the business record together with relevant conversations and documents. I can follow its sources to understand the issue. I spend less time piecing together the full picture.
- **0:50** That's the shift from finding records to understanding the business. My app stays the system of record. Copilot helps me decide what needs attention and where to act next.

### 3. Write back to your business app (1:00-1:30)

- **1:00** I need the next step analyzed and recorded in Dataverse. With the Business apps plugin enabled and connected, I can delegate that work to Cowork instead of doing it myself.
- **1:10** Cowork proposes a focused update. I review the change before it saves within my app's permissions. The next step now belongs with the team's business record in Dataverse.
- **1:20** The follow-up is saved on the launch record. The evidence task is still blocked. A saved plan doesn't resolve it. I need visibility into progress and next actions.

### 4. Bring the next review to you (1:30-2:00)

- **1:30** Rather than repeat that review myself, I can schedule it in Cowork. The briefing checks progress and flags work that needs attention. Follow-up becomes part of my routine.
- **1:40** Work IQ brings business records and workplace context into the review. Cowork uses that connected understanding to explain the situation and propose next steps. I can focus on decisions.
- **1:50** Cowork can deliver the update to my email. The context and proposed actions come to me. I stay in the flow of work instead of assembling another report.

## Close

> Bring business context together. Save reviewed updates in Dataverse and schedule follow-up. Explore the worldwide preview rollout in Microsoft Copilot and Cowork. Put your app to work.

## Keep it honest

- Edited and sped up with presenter approval. This is not real-time response
  performance. Four steps are 30 seconds each; the close is 10 seconds.
- Narration is a synthetic timing preview. Business content is not rewritten.
- Chat is a live review of a saved answer. The native scheduled email is
  earlier verified footage, not another send during this edit.
- The demonstrated one-time scheduled run required attended read approvals.
  Future reviews describe the scheduling capability, not proof of unattended
  recurring execution. Connected context does not imply zero indexing latency,
  offline access, or access beyond existing permissions.
- This revision is voiced continuously through the full 2:10, with natural
  sentence pauses rather than deliberate gaps between cues or a silent close.
  The preview fits speech to cue windows without changing the picture speed.
- The current script contains 368 words, down from 433. Average delivery
  is about 170 words per minute, 15 percent slower than the previous edition.
  Repeated supporting phrases were trimmed without changing the story.
- The narrative paraphrases the Microsoft blog's shared business understanding,
  system-of-record, governed-action, and flow-of-work themes. Business skills
  and additional agent experiences in the blog were not demonstrated here.
- The opening Chat screen is navigation only. Section 2 reviews the successful
  saved conversation; it does not splice a different run into new generation.
- The closing diagram is shown on Microsoft Learn after a recorded page
  scroll. It illustrates product context, not additional demonstrated agents.
  An editorial zoom enlarges the diagram.
- Worldwide availability is described as a preview rollout, not guaranteed
  completion in every tenant. Microsoft's announcement says rollout continues
  through October; licensing, permissions, and setup still apply.
